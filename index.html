Method and limits

Method

  • Marketing's answers were read from the online sheet (Nov 19, 2026, 58 rows).
  • Every proof file was re-read: text PDFs by text extraction; image-only PDFs rendered page by page to pictures and read; PNG screenshots and Google Drive files read directly.
  • For the resume, the role and our assessment result, the original 'Assessment Support' request mail, the resume attachment and our expert's result mail in Outlook were used.
  • One category per row, based on what the proof shows. 'Answer vs proof' says whether Marketing's answer matches that proof.
  • Auto-graded = a platform test scored by software (HackerRank, Codility, CodeSignal, Coderbyte, Glider, Wonderlic, Criteria Corp, Spark Hire, SHL, Talogy, Google and Amazon online assessments, McKinsey Solve and similar games). Not auto-graded = recorded video or AI interviews, take-home projects, a client's own pre-screen questionnaire, a live presentation.
  • Role match = the resume read against the job title or description: Match, Partial or Mismatch. It is a reading, not a score.

Limits

  • Most request mails have no job description, so resume fit is against the job title and the real role named in the client's mail.
  • Contact details marked 'in the invite' come from the invite attached to our request mail; the others are visible in the proof file itself.
  • Several assessments were expert-assisted, so a 'Passed' result says little about the candidate's own performance.
  • Reasons for rejection are only what the client wrote. Where a client gave none, none is assumed.
  • The sheet keeps changing as Marketing edits it; counts are for Nov 19, 2026.
