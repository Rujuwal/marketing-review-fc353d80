METHODOLOGY / CONTROLS

Definitions, limitations and decision standard

Definitions

Tracker conversion: later same-candidate/same-client interview event as used by the source deck, including interviews done, scheduled and cancelled after scheduling. This is not a measure of placements.

Next-stage conversion: documented employer invitation or advancement to a subsequent selection stage after an earlier assessment. Only confirmed movement is counted; an interview invitation is not an interview held.

Verified placement: signed offer/acceptance or equivalent authoritative placement record. An offer claim alone is insufficient.

Internal pass: the Technical Team's internal result mail labels an assessment “Passed,” with or without a formal employer score.

Employer assessment pass: employer explicitly confirms the assessment test was passed, without necessarily advancing the application.

Employer rejection: rejection email or portal outcome for the application; the assessment cause remains unknown unless expressly stated.

Auto-generated proof: invitation, system acknowledgment or platform report, not a final hiring decision.

Unverified: absent, unrelated, or inadequate client-side documentation.

Key limitations and safeguards

  • Tracker source is the Nov 1–16 assessment review deck (Nov 16 snapshot): 121 total, 7 converted, 63 aged not converted and 51 awaiting maturity. The separate Nov 19 Marketing workbook has 58 audited rows.
  • The workbook summarizes reviews of screenshots, emails and files. Those underlying primary files are not included here for re-verification.
  • Some employer feedback is generic. Do not infer assessment failure, recruiter intent or culpability from an absence of detail.
  • 58 rows are assessment records, not necessarily 58 unique candidates or client decisions; IBM screenshots appear on two rows. Of the 60 rows sent to Marketing on Nov 16, 58 match the Nov 19 sheet by candidate, client, assessment type and result. 2 rows are no longer on the sheet: Amulya Sangllo / DV Commodities (Technical, Passed, Oct 30) and Sai Charan Nimmagadda / Whataburger (Behavioral, Unknown, Oct 29). The reason for their removal is not recorded.
  • The tracker’s 63 aged without conversion is not the same as an initially missing pass/fail result; it also does not prove employer rejection. Candidate follow-ups do not establish Marketing reached a hiring decision-maker.
  • Resume-job fit labels are qualitative reviewer judgments, not employer-issued rejection reasons.
